Property development · Tauranga

Property development construction input for Tauranga sites.

For selected infill and multi-unit opportunities, DELVIK connects site information, buildability, scope definition and delivery planning so important assumptions are tested before construction commitments.

Discuss your developmentCheck project readiness

Feasibility is more than a floor-area calculation.

A development review should consider access, earthworks, retaining, services, staging, temporary works, repeated details, procurement and the level of documentation available. These items can materially affect delivery even when the planning yield looks attractive.

DELVIK’s role is builder-side: identify construction questions, help define the information needed for pricing and align the proposed sequence with the project’s commercial objectives.

Useful inputs

  • Site and title information
  • Planning or concept assessment
  • Survey, geotechnical and civil information
  • Concept or developed drawings
  • Target programme and exit strategy

From assumptions to a delivery brief

Site constraints

Buildability

Identify complex details, repeated scopes and opportunities for more consistent delivery.

Scope definition

Separate confirmed information, provisional allowances and client or consultant responsibilities.

Delivery planning

Consider staging, trade flow, procurement and inspection requirements against the target programme.

Design and build in Tauranga with construction input from the start.

One accountable construction pathway.

Design and build works best when responsibilities are explicit. DELVIK can contribute builder-side coordination while architectural, engineering and specialist design remains with appropriately appointed consultants.

Early review considers site constraints, structure, material interfaces, consent information, procurement and the decisions that have the greatest effect on cost and programme.

Typical stages

  • Client brief and site information
  • Concept buildability review
  • Developed design coordination
  • Consent and pricing information
  • Construction and handover planning

Decisions made at the right time

Brief

Clarify priorities, must-haves, budget expectations and the decision-making structure.

Design

Review construction sequence, interfaces, access and material implications while options remain open.

Commercial definition

Align drawings, specifications, allowances and exclusions before trade commitments.

Delivery

Carry the agreed scope and communication process through site execution and handover.

Suitable Tauranga projects

The pathway can suit new homes, substantial renovations, extensions and selected light-commercial work where the client wants construction input before documentation is complete.

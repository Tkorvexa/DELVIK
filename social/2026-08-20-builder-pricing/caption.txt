A useful building price starts with useful project information.

A feasibility allowance, preliminary estimate and formal quote are not the same thing. The more clearly the site, drawings, specifications, specialist information, consent status and programme are defined, the more useful a builder’s response can be.

You do not need every detail resolved before the first conversation. You do need to be clear about the current project stage—and the type of pricing response you are asking for.

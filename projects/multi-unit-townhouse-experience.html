Nine-unit townhouse development

Repeatable carpentry quality across multiple homes.

This record reflects Thiago Cortes’ project-team construction experience and does not claim the development as a sole-contract DELVIK project.

Role and contribution

Thiago contributed residential carpentry across a nine-townhouse programme where consistent set-out, sequencing and closeout mattered from one unit to the next.

Delivery priorities

Multi-unit work rewards repeatable checks and early feedback. Small deviations can multiply across units, while delayed decisions can disrupt several trade sequences. The experience strengthened programme awareness and a systematic approach to workmanship.

How this informs DELVIK

The experience supports builder-side review for Tauranga property development, where repeated details, access, staging and procurement need to be considered before construction.

Residential experience · Queenstown

High-spec residential carpentry and site-delivery experience.

This project record describes Thiago Cortes’ employed and project-team experience before and alongside establishing DELVIK. It is not presented as a sole-contract DELVIK project.

Role and contribution

Thiago contributed hands-on carpentry, sequencing, quality checks and crew support across detailed residential work. Acting lead-hand responsibilities developed practical experience coordinating daily activities while maintaining finish expectations.

Delivery priorities

High-spec work places pressure on set-out, weather-tightness, material protection, trade interfaces and the timing of client or consultant decisions. The experience reinforced the value of checking work before it is covered and recording issues early.

Environment
High-spec residential construction
Contribution
Carpentry, sequencing, quality checks and crew support
Transferable value
Detailed planning and visible quality control

How this informs DELVIK

The lessons carry into DELVIK’s approach to new homes in Tauranga: define information early, plan trade interfaces, protect completed work and keep responsibility close to the site.

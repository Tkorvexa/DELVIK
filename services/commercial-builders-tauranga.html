Construction planned around the operating environment.

Commercial upgrades often involve existing services, public or staff access, restricted working hours, neighbouring tenants and fixed operational dates. DELVIK reviews those constraints with the physical scope before committing to a programme.

Clear work zones, responsibilities, shutdown requirements and decision dates help trades coordinate efficiently and reduce disruption to the client’s operation.

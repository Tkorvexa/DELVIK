House extensions · Tauranga

House extensions in Tauranga that connect old and new properly.

An extension succeeds at the junction with the existing home. DELVIK coordinates structure, roofing, cladding, waterproofing, services, temporary protection and the sequence needed to keep the property safe during construction.

Assess your extensionCheck project readiness

More space without losing control of the existing building.

Extension pricing depends on more than the new floor area. The builder must understand the existing structure, levels, drainage, services, ground conditions and how the building will remain weatherproof while openings are formed.

DELVIK reviews the available survey, architectural and engineering information, confirms access and identifies temporary works or investigations that should be included before a programme is committed.

Key information

  • Existing and proposed plans
  • Engineering and geotechnical information
  • Site access and occupation requirements
  • Consent status
  • Finish and service expectations

Extension delivery priorities

Structural connection

Coordinate new foundations, framing and load paths with the actual existing construction.

Water management

Plan roof, cladding, flashing and opening sequences to protect the home during and after construction.

Services

Confirm how power, plumbing, drainage, heating and communications cross the old-to-new boundary.

Client access

Set realistic expectations for occupation, temporary facilities, noise and security.

Extension work across Tauranga

DELVIK considers extensions in Tauranga, Papamoa, Mount Maunganui and selected Western Bay of Plenty locations. Coastal exposure, tight access and differences between original documentation and existing construction are considered during review.

For work largely contained inside the existing envelope, see home renovations Tauranga. For a completely new residence, see new home builders Tauranga.

Common questions

Do extensions require consent?

Many structural and building-envelope extensions do. Your designer or council can confirm the pathway for the specific scope.

Can DELVIK price concept plans?

We can review them and identify missing information, but reliable trade pricing usually requires developed drawings and defined finishes.

# DELVIK Building Insights editorial calendar

## Publishing standard

- Publish one useful English (New Zealand) article each week.
- Aim for 900–1,500 words and answer a real pre-construction question clearly.
- Use current primary New Zealand sources, such as Building Performance/MBIE, WorkSafe New Zealand, legislation.govt.nz and the relevant council.
- Link every material regulatory or technical claim to its source.
- Do not invent projects, testimonials, performance figures, credentials or customer outcomes.
- Include an author, publication date, Article structured data and a general-information disclaimer.
- Add the article to `/insights/index.html`, the home page when appropriate, and `sitemap.xml`.
- Validate HTML, structured data, internal links and JavaScript before publishing.

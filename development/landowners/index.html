Start with feasibility, not assumptions.

A large site is not automatically a good development. DELVIK first tests planning potential, access, services, site constraints, likely product, market demand, construction complexity and commercial viability. Only sites that justify deeper investigation progress.

Sites we want to see

Under-utilised residential land

Older dwellings on larger sections, rear land, corner sites and properties where current use may not reflect permitted density.

Consented or stalled developments

Projects with resource consent, building consent or design work completed but requiring capital, construction capability or a new delivery partner.

Multi-unit potential

Sites that may support duplexes, terraces, townhouses, apartments or other residential intensification subject to planning and feasibility.

Landowner partnership opportunities

Owners who want to explore participating in a project rather than automatically selling the land outright.

Mixed-use and conversion

Selected commercial, mixed-use or brownfield assets where redevelopment may unlock a stronger use.

Off-market introductions

Agents, planners, architects, surveyors, advisers and owners are welcome to introduce opportunities before public marketing.

How the DELVIK Land Partnership Program works

1. Submit the property

Send the address and basic site information. Plans, LIM, title, geotech and consent documents help but are not required for the first screen.

2. Initial development screen

We review visible planning settings, access, services, hazards, likely density, market context and obvious buildability constraints.

3. Feasibility & due diligence

Strong opportunities can progress into concept yield, cost, revenue, finance, planning and technical investigation with the appropriate professional team.

4. Select the commercial pathway

Depending on the project, this may be a conventional purchase, conditional acquisition, deferred settlement or a separately documented project-specific partnership.

Current geographic focus

National ambition. Disciplined site selection.

DELVIK will assess selected opportunities throughout New Zealand. Current priority markets include Auckland, Hamilton and the wider Waikato, Tauranga and Bay of Plenty, Wellington, Christchurch and Canterbury, Queenstown Lakes and other locations where fundamentals justify the project.

Being in a target city does not make a site suitable. Every opportunity must earn its place through feasibility.

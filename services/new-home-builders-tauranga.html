Before construction

A useful price starts with a buildable scope.

Before pricing, DELVIK reviews the drawings, specifications, site access, ground information, services, structural interfaces and selections that materially affect construction. The objective is to expose uncertainty early rather than bury it inside allowances.

If plans are still developing, early builder input can identify difficult junctions, sequencing risks and missing information before consent or procurement. If consent is approved, the review shifts toward scope completeness, trade pricing, lead times and a realistic start programme.

Information to send

  • Current architectural and engineering drawings
  • Site address or lot information
  • Specifications and finish schedule
  • Consent status and target timing
  • Known budget range

How DELVIK approaches a new build

Pre-construction

Clarify inclusions, exclusions and assumptions; review buildability; coordinate pricing inputs; and establish procurement priorities.

Site delivery

Coordinate trades, inspections, safety, quality hold points and client decisions against a visible construction programme.

Cost visibility

Record changes and decisions as they occur so the commercial position remains understandable throughout the build.

Handover

Complete inspections, close defects, compile records and provide a structured transition into the finished home.

Common questions

Can DELVIK review plans before consent?

Yes. Early review can identify construction risks and scope gaps while the design is still easier to change.

Do you provide square-metre rates?

Not as a substitute for reviewing the actual site, drawings and specification. A useful budget needs project-specific information.

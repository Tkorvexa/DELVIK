# DELVIK Building Insights editorial calendar

## Publishing standard

- Publish one useful English (New Zealand) article each week.
- Aim for 900–1,500 words and answer a real pre-construction question clearly.
- Use current primary New Zealand sources, such as Building Performance/MBIE, WorkSafe New Zealand, legislation.govt.nz and the relevant council.
- Link every material regulatory or technical claim to its source.
- Do not invent projects, testimonials, performance figures, credentials or customer outcomes.
- Include an author, publication date, Article structured data and a general-information disclaimer.
- Add the article to `/insights/index.html`, the home page when appropriate, and `sitemap.xml`.
- Validate HTML, structured data, internal links and JavaScript before publishing.

## Instagram connection — @delvik_build

Each weekly Insight should generate one coordinated Instagram content package:

- One carousel (6–8 slides) that explains the central question in plain New Zealand English.
- One caption with a clear hook, useful summary, soft call to action and link-in-bio direction to the full Insight.
- Three Stories: question/poll, key takeaway and link/CTA.
- When suitable, one short Reel script (20–40 seconds) using site footage, plans, materials or a team explanation.
- Do not present generic stock imagery as Delvik project work. Only identify a project as Delvik's when this can be verified.

### Recommended weekly rhythm

- Monday: confirm the week's question, audience and primary source set.
- Tuesday: prepare and review the long-form Insight.
- Wednesday: publish the Insight on the Delvik website.
- Thursday: publish the Instagram carousel and caption.
- Friday: publish Stories and capture recurring questions for future Insights.

Track reach, profile visits, saves, shares, link clicks and qualified enquiries. Use saves and qualified enquiries as stronger editorial signals than likes alone.

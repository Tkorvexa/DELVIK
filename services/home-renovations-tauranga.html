Home renovations · Tauranga

Home renovations in Tauranga planned around the unknowns.

Renovations connect new work to an existing building. DELVIK focuses on investigation, temporary protection, access, sequencing and clear change control so hidden conditions do not quietly become unmanaged cost and delay.

Discuss your renovationCheck project readiness

Define what is known before opening the building.

A useful renovation scope separates confirmed work from items that need investigation. Existing structure, services, moisture history, access, occupied areas and the junction between old and new can all influence the programme.

DELVIK reviews available drawings, photographs and site information, then identifies where inspection or specialist input is needed. This creates a clearer basis for pricing and a more disciplined way to manage discoveries once work begins.

Typical renovation work

  • Structural and internal alterations
  • Kitchen and living-area reconfiguration
  • Weather-tightness and exterior interfaces
  • Staged upgrades in occupied homes
  • Coordination with engineers and designers

The controls that matter

Existing-condition review

Record visible conditions and identify areas that require opening-up, measurement or specialist advice.

Protection and access

Plan dust, weather, security, temporary services and safe separation where clients remain in the property.

Change management

Document discoveries, options and approvals before additional work proceeds wherever practicable.

Old-to-new quality

Pay close attention to structural, cladding, roofing, waterproofing and finish junctions.

Renovating in Tauranga and coastal suburbs

DELVIK assesses renovation projects in Tauranga, Mount Maunganui, Papamoa and selected Western Bay of Plenty areas. Exposure, age of the home, site access and the extent of structural change influence the investigation and delivery plan.

Common questions

Can we live in the home?

Sometimes. The answer depends on services, dust, safety, weather protection and the sequence. This should be agreed before pricing.

What if hidden damage is found?

The condition is recorded, the impact explained and approval obtained before the associated variation proceeds, except where urgent protection is required.

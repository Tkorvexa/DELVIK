# Backlinks And Google Business Profile Playbook

## Official guidance references

- Google SEO Starter Guide: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide
- Helpful content guidance: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- LocalBusiness structured data: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business
- Google Business Profile local ranking help: https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091

## Google Business Profile setup checklist

Use a real business name, real phone number and real service details only.

### Core profile

- Primary category: choose the closest real category to `Builder` or `Construction company`.
- Secondary categories: add only categories that match actual services.
- Service areas: Tauranga, Bay of Plenty, Auckland if truly served, and other real areas only.
- Business description: include builder, renovation, residential and project delivery language naturally.
- Hours: keep accurate and updated.
- Website URL: point to the main build page.
- Appointment/contact URL: point to `contact-build.html`.

### Photos and media

- Upload logo.
- Upload cover photo.
- Upload site photos every month.
- Upload before/after or progress photos from real jobs.
- Use geo-relevant captions when posting updates.

### Reviews

- Ask every satisfied client for a review.
- Ask them to mention service type and location naturally.
- Reply to every review with location and service context.

Example reply:

`Thank you for trusting DELVIK Build on your Tauranga renovation. We appreciate the opportunity to deliver the project and keep communication clear from start to finish.`

### Posts

Post weekly or fortnightly:

- project update
- completed work
- certification update
- safety/process story
- local service area story

## Backlink strategy

Focus on relevant and legitimate local backlinks.

### High-priority backlink sources

1. NZ trade and construction directories
2. Tauranga and Bay of Plenty business directories
3. Chamber of Commerce listings
4. Supplier and subcontractor partner pages
5. Industry memberships and accreditation profiles
6. Local sponsorship or community pages
7. Project features on partner/developer websites

### Citation consistency

Keep these identical everywhere:

- business name
- phone number
- email
- website
- service areas

## Avoid

- bought backlinks
- spam guest posts
- random global directories
- fake reviews
- keyword stuffing in the business name

## 90-day execution order

### Week 1-2

- Finish Google Business Profile
- Submit sitemap to Search Console
- Standardize NAP across all listings

### Week 3-6

- Publish 2 to 4 location/service pages
- Get 5 to 10 strong citations
- Request 5 new reviews

### Week 7-12
